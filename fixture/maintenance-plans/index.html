What no plan covers.

Every maintenance contract in this trade has an exclusion list. Most of them are in an appendix. Ours is here, because finding it after a failure is how relationships end.

  • Damage from misuse, vandalism or water ingress into the shaft.
  • Statutory thorough examination itself, which your insurer's engineer must carry out independently.
  • Builder's work: shaft structure, lighting circuits, mains supply, machine-room ventilation.
  • Obsolete proprietary control boards where the manufacturer no longer supplies them and no equivalent exists.
  • Recovery of a lift after a flood, fire or long-term shutdown — quoted separately as a reinstatement.
