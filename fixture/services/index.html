Services

Everything we do to a lift, and what it costs you in downtime.

Six service lines. Each one has a defined scope, a defined exclusion list and a named engineer. Nothing here is sold as a bundle you cannot read.

  1. Survey

    Half a day per unit. We open the controller, read the fault log, check the rope condition and photograph what we find, including the things the last firm left.

  2. Written scope

    A per-unit scope with visit frequency, inclusions, exclusions and the parts we expect to need in the first eighteen months.

  3. Planned visits

    Fixed dates published a year ahead. Your building manager knows in January which Tuesdays we are on site in November.

  4. Quarterly report

    Response times, jobs completed, parts fitted, and every window we missed. Sent whether or not the quarter looked good.

Service lines

Priced per unit, per month. Call-out labour outside the contracted hours is charged at the rate printed in your agreement, never at a rate discovered afterwards.

01

Planned maintenance

Scheduled examination of door gear, brakes, suspension, safety gear and controller logs against a written checklist.

  • 4, 6 or 12 visits a year
  • Signed report left on site
  • Lubrication and adjustment included
02

24-hour call-out

A duty controller on a real phone. Entrapments outrank every other job on the board without exception.

  • 60-minute entrapment attendance
  • Callback with engineer name in 10 minutes
  • Missed window means no charge
03

Repairs and spares

Component-level repair with the lead time quoted before the order goes in, not after the lift is already stripped.

  • Door operators and gear
  • Contactors, boards and drives
  • Ropes, sheaves and safety gear
04

Modernisation

Staged upgrades that keep at least one car running in a two-lift building throughout the works.

  • Controller and drive replacement
  • Car interior and fixtures
  • Landing door refurbishment
05

Thorough examination support

We do not carry out your statutory examination — that is your insurer's engineer. We prepare the unit, attend, and close the defects.

  • Pre-examination preparation visit
  • Attendance on the day
  • Defect closure with evidence
06

Accessibility lifts

Platform lifts and stairlifts in public buildings, where an out-of-service unit is a legal problem as well as an inconvenience.

  • Platform and step lifts
  • Battery and controller servicing
  • Priority response as standard

Parts, and why we hold them.

The slowest part of most repairs is not the work. It is waiting for a component that a manufacturer ships in eleven weeks. We hold a stocked kit for the traction units that make up most of our estate, which is why our first-visit fix rate is a number we are willing to print.

A stocked kit only helps if it matches the unit. Where a building runs equipment we do not stock for, we say so in the survey rather than discovering it during an entrapment.
