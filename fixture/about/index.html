About us

A lift firm small enough that you know who is coming.

Meridian was set up in 2004 by two engineers who were tired of being the third contractor sent to the same fault. Twenty-one years later the rota is bigger and the principle has not moved.

How we work

Every building has a named engineer and a named deputy. They keep the same buildings for years, because the value of this job is almost entirely in knowing what a particular controller does when it is cold and what the last firm bodged in 2011.

We do not subcontract call-outs. If a Meridian van arrives, a Meridian engineer is driving it, and they have your building's history on the tablet beside them.

We turn work down. If a building runs equipment we cannot get parts for, we say so at survey rather than signing a contract and finding out during an entrapment. That costs us contracts every year and it is still the right call.

Safety and competence

Lift work kills people who take shortcuts on the top of a car. Our rules are boring and we do not flex them for a client's deadline.

  • No engineer works on a car top alone outside working hours.
  • Every apprentice is supervised for a full 36 months, with no exceptions for busy quarters.
  • Isolation is proved, not assumed, and the proof is photographed.
  • Near misses are logged and circulated the same week, with the engineer's name removed.
