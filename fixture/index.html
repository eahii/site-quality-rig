What we do

Three contracts, one engineer who knows your building.

Most of our work is unglamorous and scheduled. The rest is the phone ringing at 02:40 because a car has stopped between floors with someone inside.

01

Fixed-date visits, not "sometime this quarter". Door gear, brakes, ropes, controller logs and safety circuits, checked against a written schedule you get a copy of.

How a call-out runs

The commitments we will put in a contract.

Response times are the part of this trade most often promised and least often measured. Ours are written into the contract, logged per job, and reported back to you every quarter whether the quarter went well or not.

  • An entrapment is attended ahead of every other job on the board.
  • The duty controller calls you back within ten minutes with an engineer name and an arrival window.
  • If we miss a contracted response window, the visit is not charged — you do not have to ask.
  • Every visit leaves a signed report on site and a copy in your portal the same day.
Entrapment
Attend within 60 minutes, any hour, every day of the year.

Attend by the end of the next working day.

Clients

Buildings that stopped thinking about their lifts.

Meridian holds contracts on residential blocks, two hospitals, a university campus and a cold-store distribution site. The mix matters: a goods lift in a chilled warehouse fails differently from a passenger lift in a 1970s tower, and an engineer who only sees one of them gets slower at the other.
